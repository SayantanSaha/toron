# TC-037 - Brotli and Zstandard Response Compression Verification

## Test Objectives

1. Verify `Accept-Encoding: br` triggers Brotli compression, sets `Content-Encoding: br`, and payload decompresses accurately to the original string.
2. Verify `Accept-Encoding: zstd` triggers Zstandard compression, sets `Content-Encoding: zstd`, and payload decompresses accurately to the original string.
3. Verify algorithm quality factors (e.g. `gzip;q=1.0, br;q=0.5` selects `gzip`, while `br, gzip` defaults to `br`).
4. Verify `zstd` takes top precedence over `br` and `gzip` when quality factors are equal.
5. Verify payloads under `min_length` bypass compression.
6. Verify binary MIME types (e.g. `image/png`) bypass compression.
7. Verify WebSocket `101 Switching Protocols` bypass compression.
