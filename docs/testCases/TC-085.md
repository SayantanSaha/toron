# TC-085 - Verification of Kubernetes Ingress Route Scoping and Sensitive Endpoint Boundary Protection

## Objective

Verify that `TranslateIngress` in `pkg/ingress/translator.go` rejects unhosted root routes, blocks shadowing of internal and administrative endpoints, canonicalizes traversal sequences, and logs security rejection warnings.

## Test Specifications

### TC-085-01: Rejection of Unhosted Root Path
- **Setup**: Ingress with `host: ""` and `path: "/"`.
- **Expected**: `TranslateIngress` rejects the rule, returning `nil, false`.

### TC-085-02: Allowance of Hosted Root Path
- **Setup**: Ingress with `host: "web.example.com"` and `path: "/"`.
- **Expected**: `TranslateIngress` accepts the rule, returning valid discovered route for `web.example.com`.

### TC-085-03: Rejection of `/internal` and `/internal/*` Shadowing
- **Setup**: Ingress with `path: "/internal"` and `path: "/internal/dashboard"`.
- **Expected**: `TranslateIngress` rejects both rules, returning `nil, false`.

### TC-085-04: Rejection of `/api/status` Shadowing
- **Setup**: Ingress with `path: "/api/status"`.
- **Expected**: `TranslateIngress` rejects the rule.

### TC-085-05: Rejection of Unhosted `/health` and `/metrics`
- **Setup**: Ingress with `host: ""` and `path: "/health"`.
- **Expected**: `TranslateIngress` rejects the unhosted probe route.

### TC-085-06: Path Traversal Canonicalization
- **Setup**: Ingress with `path: "/app/../internal"`.
- **Expected**: Path canonicalizes to `/internal` and is rejected.
