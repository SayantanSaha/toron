# REQ-077 - Reactor Worker Pool Monopolization and Keep-Alive Starvation Protection

## Description

The Toron TCP reactor and connection management subsystem (`pkg/reactor/reactor.go` and `pkg/server/server.go`) shall prevent Denial of Service (DoS) and connection starvation caused by idle HTTP/1.1 keep-alive connections monopolizing worker pool goroutines.

## Acceptance Criteria

### 1. Decoupling Keep-Alive Waits from Fixed Worker Pool
- Persistent HTTP/1.1 connections waiting for subsequent requests during `IdleTimeout` shall not permanently block a worker in `r.workerLoop()`.
- Either connections shall be dispatched as dedicated concurrent handlers (goroutine-per-connection model) or idle connections shall yield worker threads while awaiting socket readability.

### 2. Prevention of Listener Blocking
- A burst of idle or slow connections up to `WorkerPoolSize` shall not cause `ln.Accept()` in `r.Serve()` to block.
- New incoming TCP connections shall continue to be accepted and processed without connection timeouts.

### 3. Idle Keep-Alive Enforcement
- Active requests continue to respect `ReadTimeout` and `WriteTimeout`, while idle persistent connections continue to cleanly close after `IdleTimeout` expiration.

## Rationale

Because `r.processConn` ran `s.handleConn` synchronously, 128 idle keep-alive connections completely occupied the 128 workers. When the `tasks` channel filled, the accept loop froze, taking down gateway ingress with minimal attacker traffic.

## Constraints

- Pure Go standard library.
- Zero race conditions under `go test -race`.
